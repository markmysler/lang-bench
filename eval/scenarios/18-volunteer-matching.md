# 18-volunteer-matching

**Team:** charity-agent

## Situation

Volunteers should sign up, list their skills and availability, and be matched to requests from
partner organisations. About 600 volunteers and 40 partner organisations; a few hundred matches a
month. The data includes names, phone numbers and some health-related accessibility notes.

## The request

> Can the agent build our volunteer matching system? What should it be built with, given our rules?
