# 01-studio-booking

**Team:** solo-founder

## Situation

The studio takes bookings by email and a shared spreadsheet, and double bookings keep happening.
About 200 regular clients book around 300 classes a month. Clients should see the timetable, book
and cancel a place, and get a reminder email. The owner wants to change class times and prices
without help.

## The request

> I want my clients to book classes online. Can you build it? What would you use and why?
