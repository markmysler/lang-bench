# 14-volunteer-rota

**Team:** gov-nonprofit

## Situation

The non-profit coordinates 150 volunteers across food banks. It needs a rota: volunteers sign up
for shifts, coordinators see gaps, and everyone gets reminders. It stores names, phone numbers and
availability. A contractor will build it in a month; whoever looks after it next will be someone
else.
